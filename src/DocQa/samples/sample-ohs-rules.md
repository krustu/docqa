# Sample: Workplace Safety Training Rules (fictional demo document)

This file is invented demo content, not a real regulation. It only exists so the app has something to search on first launch.

## Initial training

Every new employee must complete the initial safety training before starting work. The training lasts at least 8 hours and ends with a short written test. An employee who does not pass the test must repeat the training within 14 days.

## Periodic training

Office workers repeat the safety training every 6 years. Workers in physical or hazardous roles repeat it every 3 years. The employer keeps a record of every completed training, including the date, the trainer and the test result.

## Certificates

After a successful test the employee receives a certificate with the employee name, the training type, the completion date and the expiry date. The certificate must be stored in the personnel file. A scan of the certificate should be uploaded to the company system within 5 working days.

## Accident reporting

Any workplace accident must be reported to the supervisor immediately. The supervisor fills in the accident report within 24 hours and sends it to the safety officer. Serious accidents must also be reported to the labour inspectorate without delay.
